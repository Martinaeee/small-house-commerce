# 1. Why LUWAG

**LUWAG** is the root of the Tagalog word **maluwag** — *spacious, roomy,
loose, at ease*. It carries exactly two meanings a small-space furniture
shop wants:

1. **Space** — *maluwag ang bahay*: the home feels roomy.
2. **Budget** — *maluwag sa budget*: affordable, no money stress.

A third, softer meaning: *luwag lang* — *relax, take it easy* — the
emotional payoff of a calm, uncluttered home.

It is short (5 letters), all-Latin, pronounceable for foreigners
(LOO-wag), globally clearable, and every key asset — both `.ph` domains,
Class 20 trademark, the full social handle set — was verified free on
2026-09-14. Clearance evidence and rejected candidates are recorded in
the project naming memory; do not re-open naming unless this document is
superseded.

## 1.1 Known risks (must be managed, not hidden)

| Risk | Fact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Cebuano/Bisaya "ladle" | In Cebuano, **luwag** can mean a cooking ladle (syn. sandok); bamboo "luwag" listings exist on Lazada nationwide. | First 12 months always use the descriptor **LUWAG Living**; never use spoon/bowl/kitchen iconography; launch and concentrate media in Luzon (NCR) first; run the speaker test in Appendix A before any Visayas/Mindanao spend. |
| Sounds like *lugaw* | Lugaw = rice porridge. | Do not joke about food/porridge in brand content; wordmark and pronunciation guide anchor LOO-wag. |
| Bare root is formal | *luwag* alone is the dictionary root; everyday Tagalog uses *maluwag*. | The wordmark may stand alone visually, but the first sentence of every landing/profile context pairs it with English: "LUWAG — small-space furniture, made roomy." |
| Squatters are active on short Tagalog `.ph` words | tahana.ph registered 2026-07, hinga.ph 2026-08. | **Domains must be registered today, by the founder.** |

# 2. Brand Platform

## 2.1 Positioning statement

For Filipino renters and first-homeowners living in 25–40 sqm condos and
apartments who want a home that feels calm instead of cramped, **LUWAG
Living** sells space-smart furniture, homeware and storage at ₱199–₱3,999.
Unlike generic marketplace sellers and expensive malls, every product is
picked for small-room dimensions, renter-safe assembly and cash-on-
delivery convenience — so your space feels **maluwag**, and so does your
budget.

## 2.2 Target customer

- 24–38, Metro Manila first (NCR → Luzon), renting a condo/apartment or
  newly moved into a small home.
- Shops via Facebook/TikTok on mobile, pays COD, judges by real-room
  photos and dimensions, not studio sets.
- Wants the Muji/IKEA look but not IKEA prices, shipping pain or sizes
  built for 80 sqm homes.

## 2.3 Brand promise

**A home that feels bigger than its floor plan, without blowing the
budget.**

## 2.4 Brand pillars

1. **Space-smart first** — every SKU must justify itself on dimensions and
   function in a real small room.
2. **Honestly affordable** — fair fixed prices, no fake discounts;
   *maluwag sa budget* is a claim we can prove.
3. **Calm and warm** — Japandi restraint, Filipino hospitality in the
   voice; never cold-minimalist or snobby.
4. **Renter-kind** — easy assembly, no-drill options, light enough to move
   to the next apartment.

## 2.5 Brand essence (internal, one word)

**Roominess** — in the room, in the budget, in how the customer feels.

## 3.3 Tone of voice

Warm, practical, plain-spoken. A helpful friend with good taste and a
small condo, not a magazine editor. Fluent Taglish is welcome; never
switch deep Tagalog at non-Tagalog audiences.

**Do / Don't with examples:**

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| "Fits a 2.1m condo wall — may butas pa para sa plants." (real dimensions, light Taglish) | "Curated objets for the discerning urban dweller." |
| "₱899 lang. COD pa. Walang risk." | "Cheap furniture for people on a budget." ("cheap" = low class) |
| "Assemble in 20 mins — walang drill, renter-friendly." | "Easy to assemble!" with no proof |
| "Maluwag na ang space mo, maluwag pa sa budget." | Jokes about lugaw, spoons, or kitchenware |
| Admit limits: "Not for 6-seater dinners — for 2, plus friends." | Pretend the piece solves every room |

Always pair a feeling claim with a concrete fact (a dimension, a price,
a minutes-to-assemble number).

# 4. Logo Direction

Execution to be commissioned; all three directions stay inside the
existing DESIGN_SYSTEM (Inter/Poppins, warm beige #C9A77B, deep brown
#6B4F3A, background #FAF8F5). Present all three in context: header,
app icon, TikTok avatar, packaging sticker.

### A. Open doorway / expanding room (recommended)
A minimal geometric mark: two walls + an opening that widens — a small
square shifting into a larger one, reading as a doorway or a room that
grew. Carries "spacious" without literal furniture; doubles as a favicon.

### B. Letter-monogram L as an opening bracket
The **L** formed by two generous-spaced strokes that bracket an open
space (negative space = the room). Quiet, premium, works at 16px.

### C. Wordmark + tile lockup (SHIPPED — v2, 2026-09-15)
"**luwag**" lowercase in **Poppins 700 with near-zero tracking
(0.02em)**, paired left of the text with the direction-A tile
(favicon geometry, ~1.12em tall, 0.4em gap). v0 shipped Poppins-600 at
0.42em tracking without the font actually loaded (system fallback); at
18px it read sparse/cheap. The v2 lockup follows the furniture-house
convention (IKEA/MUJI/Nitori: tight bold word carried by a symbol) and
the tile supplies the "room that grew" idea the wide spacing was trying
to express. Lockup is flush left in the header, em-sized so one size
override scales mark + word together. A custom-drawn wordmark can still
replace Poppins later without touching the tile.

**Hard no's:** lungs/breath marks (Ginhawa leftover), spoons/ladles/
bowls (Cebuano), porridge, house cliches with pitched roofs unless
abstracted into direction A, script/calligraphy, red as primary
(#D9534F stays sale-only).

# Appendix A. Cebuano/Bisaya Speaker Gut Test

**Why:** *luwag* = ladle in Cebuano. Luzon launch is unaffected; before
spending in Visayas/Mindanao, validate with **8–10 Cebuano/Bisaya
speakers** (mix Cebu City/Davao, ages 22–40), plus 2 Ilonggo and
2 Ilocano speakers as control. Run by chat voice note, 30 seconds each,
questions in order; do not explain the brand beforehand.

1. *(Play 2-sec audio: "LUWAG", LOO-wag)* "Anong unang pumapasok sa isip
   mo pag narinig ang salitang 'luwag'?" — free answer, do not prime.
2. "Ano ang ibig sabihin ng 'luwag' para sa'yo? Gamitin sa
   pangungusap." (For Cebuano speakers, ask in Cebuano: "Unsay meaning
   sa 'luwag' para nimo? Gamita sa sentence.")
3. *(Show logo mockup + text "LUWAG Living — furniture for small homes")*
   "Anong tinitinda ng shop na ito? May kakaiba, nakakatawa, o
   nakakalito ba sa pangalan para sa isang furniture shop? I-rate 1–5
   (1 = walang issue, 5 = ang awkward)."
4. *(Show tagline "Small space, big luwag.")* "Anong ibig sabihin nito
   para sa'yo? May ibang kahulugan ba na naiisip?"
5. "Bobolahin/oo-hin ka ba o oorder ka sa shop na may ganitong pangalan?"

**Pass criteria:** <20% immediately think of a ladle unaided; after
seeing context, mean awkwardness ≤ 2/5; zero vulgar/negative readings.
If failed: keep "LUWAG Living" descriptor permanent, pause Visayas
organic targeting, and reassess with a local co-name in paid creative.
